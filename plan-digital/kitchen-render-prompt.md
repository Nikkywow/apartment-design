# Prompt для рендера кухни

## Основной prompt

Warm organic modern kitchen in a 20.5 m² open-plan apartment, L-shaped layout, long leg along left wall, short return along top wall, strong spatial logic, generous circulation, cozy premium feel. Interior design in the style of a Pinterest moodboard “Свиблово”: dark walnut wood, olive green accents, warm cream/greige walls, graphite stone countertop, aged brass hardware, natural textiles, soft curves, elegant minimalism. High-end contemporary apartment kitchen, cozy and expensive, not cold. Built-in full-height fridge and freezer cabinet on left, sink and dishwasher, prep zone, induction cooktop, concealed hood, oven and microwave in top return cabinet, breakfast pantry with hidden coffee station. Large rounded dining area with a 110 cm round table, four chairs, soft natural light, warm 2700K lighting, under-cabinet task lighting, subtle ambient glow, premium materials, polished but relaxed atmosphere. Realistic interior photography, natural daylight plus warm artificial light, interior shot from a wide angle, shallow depth of field with crisp details, elegant composition, realistic shadows, photorealistic, high detail, refined styling.

### Дополнительные атрибуты

- warm organic modern, moody mid-century influence
- natural wood grain in walnut and dark oak
- muted olive tile backsplash, matte finish
- graphite compact stone or stone-look countertop
- satin brass / bronze hardware
- soft cream background with warm greige undertones
- compact but sophisticated furniture and integrated storage
- finished apartment feel, no cheap mass-market styling
- realistic proportions, accurate geometry, refined residential interior
- ultra realistic, architectural visualization, premium CG render

## Negative prompt

avoid cold white lighting, avoid sterile hospital aesthetic, avoid glossy ultra-shiny surfaces, avoid cheap IKEA look, avoid excessive clutter, avoid too much black, avoid industrial loft style, avoid fake over-stylized props, avoid distorted geometry, avoid unrealistic furniture proportions, avoid empty room, avoid harsh shadows, avoid oversaturated colors, avoid cartoonish render, avoid low detail.
